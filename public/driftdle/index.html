A particle sits in a field. Which way does it drift?

Call the guiding-center drift for an ion and an electron before the clock runs out. Then watch the real orbits.

  • 1The view looks down the magnetic field: ⊙ is B out of the screen, ⊗ into it.
  • 2Drag from the particle to aim the ion’s drift, then the electron’s. Arrows snap to 45°. Pick No drift if the guiding center stays put.
  • 3The right direction earns 100 per species and one notch off earns 40. At 10 seconds left you can buy one more minute for 50 points.

Units: B0 = 1. The electron has ¼ the ion’s mass so its drifts are big enough to see. Both start with the same kinetic energy.
